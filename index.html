New to Epic Cash?

Digital cash, private by default

Epic Cash (EPIC) is an open-source, peer-to-peer digital currency with no company in control. You do not need to own any to attend. Figures below are as stated on epiccash.com.

21 million
Fixed maximum supply, the same cap as Bitcoin.
Since 2019
Mining began in September 2019 from zero. No pre-mine, no ICO.
Private
Built on Mimblewimble: addresses and amounts are not stored on the blockchain.
3 ways to mine
CPU, GPU and ASIC hardware all secure the network.

Questions

Good to know

What time is that where I live?

The session starts at 10:00 AM in New York. The "Next session" box at the top of this page shows it in your own time zone.

How long is it, and who hosts it?

Each session lasts one hour. It is hosted by Romain Daniellou and Jose Enrique Castellón, with Max Freeman, the founder of Epic Cash, as the guest.

Do I need to register?

No. Adding the sessions to your calendar is enough. Subscribing by email is optional.

Do I need to own EPIC or be technical?

No. The updates are given in plain language and newcomers are welcome.

Is this investment advice?

No. The session is for information only. Nothing said in it is financial advice.
